How booking works

Four steps, no phone call.

Classes are included in every membership tier. You book them yourself, and the only thing we ask is that you cancel in time if plans change.

Sign in

Booking is a member action. Log in first — new to the club, start with a tier or a free guest pass.

Pick your slot

Seven days on this page, thirty in My Space. Spots left are live; a full class opens a waitlist instead.

Get the confirmation

It lands in My Space and in your calendar. Bring nothing but shoes — mats, gloves and towels are on site.

Show up

Tap in at the front desk. Doors close on the hour, so arrive five minutes early for the warm-up.

Cancellation. Free up to 6 hours before the class starts. Inside 6 hours, or a no-show, and the slot counts against your monthly allowance — it is the only way a capped class stays fair to the person on the waitlist.  ·  Waitlist. Automatic. If a spot frees up you are notified, and you have 30 minutes to take it.
