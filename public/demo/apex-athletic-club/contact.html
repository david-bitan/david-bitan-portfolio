Quick answers

Before you reach out.

Most first-time questions land in one of five buckets. If yours isn't here, the form is a two-line write-up — we'll take it from there.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — one full day, no card. Floor, pool, sauna, jacuzzi and one group class. Request a guest pass from the hero button or walk in during club hours.

Is there parking?

Valet at the entrance (members: complimentary), a 40-space lot in the back, and a covered bike rack. Metered street parking on Collins if all else fails.

Can I bring a guest?

Signature and Elite members get four guest passes a month. Basic members get two. Guests sign in at the front desk with a photo ID.

Do you take corporate accounts?

Yes. We work with 40+ Miami-based teams — flat corporate rate, direct invoicing, wellness reporting on request. Pick the corporate subject in the form.

How does cancellation work?

30 days notice at any time, no fee. The current billing cycle finishes as paid — no partial refund, no auto-rebill after the notice period.

Do you have showers & day-use lockers?

Full locker room with towels, hair products, and hair dryers included in every tier. Day-use lockers for guests, permanent lockers for Signature and Elite members.
