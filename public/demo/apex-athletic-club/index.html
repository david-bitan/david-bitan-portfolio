About APEX

A private club for people
who take training seriously.

Founded in 2019 by former Olympic strength coaches, APEX blends a heritage weight room with a modern medical wing. Small memberships, high touch — the whole team knows you.

The Floor

You won't find a spinning studio at APEX. What you'll find is a 8,000 sq ft strength floor with 12 competition-grade platforms, an Olympic lifting pit, a full sled track, and enough plates for anyone to work at the same time on a Saturday morning.

You'll also find an ocean-view lap pool, a Finnish sauna, a hot jacuzzi, a cold plunge, a recovery wing with cryotherapy, red-light beds, and manual therapy rooms — plus a staff dietitian, an on-call sports medicine doctor, and a physio who works with the Miami Heat during off-season.
